## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a commercial license if I'm using OMG internally?

**No.** Internal use (even at for-profit companies) is covered by AGPL-3.0 for free.

### Do I need a commercial license if I'm building an open source product?

**No.** If your product is AGPL-3.0 licensed (or compatible), use the free license.

### Do I need a commercial license if I'm offering OMG-as-a-Service?

**Maybe.** If you're willing to share your source code modifications under AGPL-3.0, you can use the free license. If you want to keep your modifications private, you need a commercial license.

### Can I trial the commercial license before purchasing?

**Yes.** We offer a **30-day free trial** for Team and Business licenses. Contact us to request trial access.

### What happens if my team grows beyond my license limit?

**Upgrade anytime.** We'll prorate your current subscription and upgrade you to the next tier. No service interruption.

### Can I cancel my subscription?

**Yes.** Cancel anytime. Your license remains valid through the end of your billing period, then reverts to AGPL-3.0.

### Do you offer discounts for non-profits / startups?

**Yes.** We offer:
- **50% discount** for registered non-profits (501c3 or equivalent)
- **30% discount** for startups < 2 years old with < $1M ARR

Contact us with proof of eligibility.

### Do you offer volume discounts for large enterprises?

**Yes.** Enterprise licenses (100+ developers) receive custom pricing with volume discounts. Contact us for a quote.
